    Money that checks itself

    Owners lose the most in the gap between what the till says and what is in the drawer. Mesob adds a verifier between the cashier and you, so no shift closes on trust alone.

    The cashier closes the shiftThey count the drawer and type the total. The system already knows what it should be.
    The verifier recounts by note200s, 100s, 50s, 10s and coins, counted separately from the cashier.
    Every digital payment is matchedEach Telebirr, CBE Birr and card reference is checked against the statement. A reference can only be used once.
    You see the verdictBalanced shifts are approved. Short or over shifts are flagged to the owner with a note.
