3days to ferment our injera

7days a week, 07:00 to 23:00

Our injera batter rests for three days before it meets the mitad, which gives it the sour taste and soft eyes you remember from home.

The berbere is dried, ground and blended in our own kitchen. Shiro is cooked to order in a clay pot and reaches your table still bubbling. On Wednesdays and Fridays, half the menu is fasting food, and it is some of our best.

Prices in birr, including VAT. Service charge of 10% is added at the table.

The coffee ceremony, every afternoon at four

Green beans roasted at the table, ground by hand and brewed in a clay jebena, served with popcorn and incense. Stay for all three rounds.

አቦልAbol, the first and strongest round
ቶናTona, the second, softer and slower
በረካBaraka, the third, the blessing

Daily 16:00 to 18:00 · Br 150 per person · no booking needed
